Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 24, 2026

Welcome to Lateef Builders. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide when interacting with our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when expressing an interest in obtaining information about our construction projects, services, or when contacting us. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Personal Details: Your full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Communication Data: Any messages, inquiries, or project details you submit through our contact form.
  • Usage Data: We may automatically collect anonymous information about how you interact with our website (e.g., browser type, IP address, pages visited) to improve user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to facilitate communication and provide you with better services. We use your data to:

  • Respond directly to your inquiries and consultation requests.
  • Send you updates regarding upcoming real estate and construction projects (only if you have opted in).
  • Improve the layout, performance, and functionality of our website.

3. Data Protection and Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is kept secure and is only accessible by authorized personnel at Lateef Builders for the sole purpose of customer service and project management.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use standard "cookies" to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files placed on your device to track site usage patterns. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some website features may not function as intended.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as our social media profiles on Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party platforms. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing them with your personal information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Lateef Builders reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in our business practices or legal obligations. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
