Non stop journey since 1962

Owing to its multidirectional role in various fields and its unique international location Karachi is rightly known as crown of Pakistan. M/S. LATEEF BUILDERS, a renowned name in construction, was established in 1962 by the visionary Mr. Muhammad Abdul Lateef (Late). Guided by his insight, and with a well-qualified, dedicated team, this organization grew rapidly into a trustworthy, modern, and reputable enterprise.

Our workmanship has a touch of class that satisfies our demanding clients with different sophisticated tastes, who insist on quality with economy and above all delivery on the due date and even before. Since our foundation, uncompromising commitment to excellence has remained the cornerstone of M/S. Lateef Builders.

As Karachi grows and prospers, our company is at the forefront of the city's incredible development. A focus on planning, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to excellence underpins the company's philosophy and ensures a bright future for the city's residents. M/S. Lateef Builders is shaped by the vision and insight of its founder and continues to shape Karachi’s future.
